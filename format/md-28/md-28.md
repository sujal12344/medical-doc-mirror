# Form MD-28: Permission to Import or Manufacture In-Vitro Diagnostics Without Predicate

The applicant (manufacturer and importer) must apply application for grant of permission to import / manufacture for sale or for distribution of In-vitro Diagnostics which does not have predicate medical device. This can be done by submitting an application through an identified online Medical Device portal of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in Form MD-28. 

This license is issued by the CDSCO to ensure to permission to import or manufacture for sale or for distribution of In-vitro Diagnostics which does not have predicate medical device. Once you have obtained an MD 29 License, you will be able to import or manufacture for sale or for distribution of In-vitro Diagnostics which does not have predicate medical device.

## Application Procedure

To apply for the license, the applicant must adhere to the following steps:
* Register the applicant on the CDSCO Portal in accordance with MDR 2017.
* Prepare a draft application.
* Upload mandatory documents as specified in the MD-28 checklist.
* Pay the required government fee.
* Submit the application on the online medical device portal.
* Responding to queries.
* Medical Device Advisory Committee (MDAC) review for novel products.
* Obtaining the Import License in MD-29.

## Required Documents

* Wholesale License
* A copy of a foreign manufacturing plant or registration of establishment. This copy must be notarized.
* Free Sale Certificate from GHTF
* Clinical Performance Evaluation data carried out in India and in other countries
* A copy of a letter which shows approval status in countries like the United Kingdom, United States of America, Australia, Canada, Japan, EU. The approval letter must contain the number and date.
* All the specific details of the country where this device has been sold for the past two years.

## Timeline for MD-28 Issuance by Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation

The process typically takes **2 - 3 months**.

## Key Recommendations

* An essential requirement is an appointment with an Authorized agent. An authorized agent is a person having a license to manufacture a medical device for sale/distribution or a wholesale license for the sale or distribution of medical devices.
* The applicant must check for approval in Global Harmonization Task Force (GHTF) countries like USFDA, Australia, Canada, Japan, and the EU.
* The investigational medical device must be approved in GHTF countries and sold for at least two years in that country.
* The applicant must also check whether a waiver from clinical trials is applicable.
* To get a waiver from clinical trials, the investigational medical device must comply with other conditions specified in MDR 2017.
