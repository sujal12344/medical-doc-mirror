# Form MD-24: Permission to Conduct Clinical Performance Evaluation

The applicant (manufacturer and importer) must apply application for grant of permission to conduct clinical performance evaluation of new in vitro diagnostic /medica device. This can be done by submitting an application through an identified online Medical Device portal of the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare in Form MD-24. 

This license is issued by the CDSCO to ensure to permission to conduct clinical performance evaluation of new in vitro diagnostic/ medica device. Once you have obtained an MD 25 License, you will be able to conduct clinical performance evaluation of new in vitro diagnostic / medica device.

## Application Procedure

To apply for the license, the applicant must adhere to the following steps:
* Register the applicant on the CDSCO Portal in accordance with MDR 2017.
* Prepare a draft application.
* Upload mandatory documents as specified in the MD-24 checklist.
* Pay the required government fee.
* Submit the application on the online medical device portal.
* Responding to queries.
* Medical Device Advisory Committee (MDAC) review for novel products.
* Obtaining the Import License in MD-25.

## Required Documents

* Undertaking by investigators
* Approval from the Ethics Committee
* Clinical Performance Evaluation plan
* In-house Performance Evaluation data (used to establish sensitivity, specificity, stability, reproducibility and repeatability).

## Timeline for MD-24 Issuance by Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation

The process typically takes **1 - 2 months**.

## Key Recommendations

* The applicant must have an undertaking from investigators.
* The Clinical Performance Evaluation plan should clearly state the purpose, scientific, technical or medical grounds and scope of the evaluation.
* The applicant must possess approval from an Ethics Committee registered with the Central Licensing Authority.

A few problems can be faced if the following points are left unchecked during the application process:
* The applicant must register the Clinical Performance Evaluation with the Clinical Trial Registry of India prior to enrolling the first participant for the Clinical Performance Evaluation.
* The Clinical Performance Evaluation plan must be initiated only after the registered Ethics Committee approves.
* The Clinical Performance Evaluation should be conducted per the guidelines of Good Clinical Practices and the approved Clinical Performance Evaluation plan.
